▶️ Song Queue

Current queue order: LIFO (Last-In, First-Out) (also shown under the clock). Triple-click any Queue button, fast, to switch between the two orders. Your choice is remembered.

LIFO (Last-In, First-Out) — the default. The last song you queued plays first and the first one you queued plays last, so the most recent thing you wanted to hear jumps to the front.

FIFO (First-In, First-Out) — songs play in the order you queued them: first queued plays first, last queued plays last.

Either way: once a queued song starts, it's off the queue. When the queue runs empty, normal playback just continues on from wherever that last queued song was in the list. Click a queued track's button once to add or remove just that song, and double-click any Queue button to clear the whole queue at once.

🎚️ Track Filters

Best Tracks / Good Tracks only play songs from that folder tier. Mp3 / Mp4 only play that file format (the other format is skipped, not removed). Best/Good are one-at-a-time, Mp3/Mp4 are one-at-a-time, and the two groups combine (e.g. Best + Mp3). Click an active button again to turn it off. Everything starts OFF each time the player opens; songs outside the active filter are dimmed in the list.

🔎 Search Modes

The small dropdown next to the search bar (desktop only) controls what your search actually looks at: Name matches file names only, Artist matches the creator (the artist tag and the artist folder), Lyrics matches lyrics text only, and All checks names, artists, albums and lyrics together. It remembers whatever you last picked. If you don't have any matching lyrics .txt files loaded, Lyrics and Both won't find anything extra beyond what Name already would — that's expected, not a bug.

Search forgives typos and missing punctuation (it matches each word loosely, in any order), and the Album mode searches the album tag. Click a result and the player plays down the remaining results, then goes back to the normal playlist right after the song that was playing before. Clearing the search ends the detour.

★ (Favorites) plays every song you starred, top to bottom, and switches off the Best/Good and Mp3/Mp4 filters. The counter next to PIP Console shows how many songs the active filters leave. Clicking Volume Control & Playback Speed resets speed, frequency, pitch and quality (not volume). Quality only lowers quality from the file's own level (it can't improve it); all sliders are remembered. Alarms always keep one blank template, and a ringing alarm stops itself after 15 minutes.
