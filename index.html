Arrange your files in this exact layout to unlock the Best Tracks and Good Tracks filter buttons.

📁 Music@mp3z&4  ← root folder — upload this
│
├── 📁 Music@mp3z  GOOD  Good tracks
│
├── 📁 The Best of The Best Mp3z  BEST  Best tracks
│
└── 📁 Lyrics  ← lyrics folder

⚠️ Drag-and-drop folder upload requires Chrome/Edge. Safari on iOS supports individual files only.
